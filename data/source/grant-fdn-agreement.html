Grant Agreement: After-School Tutoring Program

Grant No. HFF-26-118 · Grantor: Hartwell Family Foundation · Grantee: Eastbay Youth Literacy · Grant period: September 1, 2026 to August 31, 2027 · Grant amount: $45,000

1. Purpose

This grant supports the Grantee's After-School Tutoring Program at three community sites.

2. Use of Funds

2.1 Grant funds may be used for tutoring program staff salaries in proportion to time worked on the Program.

2.2 Grant funds may be used for tutoring books and supplies.

2.3 Grant funds may be used for snacks for participating students.

3. Restrictions

3.1 Grant funds may not be used for consultants or contractors without the Foundation's prior written approval.

3.2 Grant funds may not be used for fundraising events, alcohol, or staff entertainment.

3.3 Grant funds may not be used for general operating costs such as rent, utilities, or office supplies.

4. Records and Reporting

4.1 The Grantee shall maintain time records for staff whose salaries are charged to this grant.

4.2 The Grantee shall submit a financial report each quarter showing expenses charged to this grant.
