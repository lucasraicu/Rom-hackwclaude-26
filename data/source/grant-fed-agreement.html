Subaward Agreement: Federal Literacy Program

Subaward No. FLS-2026-0412 · Pass-through entity: State Office of Community Learning · Subrecipient: Eastbay Youth Literacy · Period of performance: July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 · Total award: $96,000

Section 1. Purpose

This subaward supports the Federal Literacy Program. The Program provides structured literacy instruction for students in grades 2 and 3 at partner elementary schools.

Section 2. Allowable Costs

2.1 Salaries and fringe benefits are allowable for staff time spent delivering or directly supervising Program instruction.

2.2 Instructional materials, books, and software licenses used in Program classrooms are allowable.

Section 3. Unallowable Costs

3.1 Costs of alcoholic beverages are unallowable under 2 CFR 200.423.

3.2 Entertainment costs, including social activities and staff celebrations, are unallowable under 2 CFR 200.438.

3.3 Costs of fundraising are unallowable under 2 CFR 200.442.

3.4 Costs of the Subrecipient's other programs, including its after-school tutoring program, may not be charged to this subaward.

3.5 General operating costs such as rent, utilities, and office supplies may not be charged directly to this subaward.

Section 4. Documentation

4.1 Charges for personnel must be based on records that accurately reflect the work performed, as required by 2 CFR 200.430(i).

4.2 Budget estimates alone do not qualify as support for charges to this subaward.

4.3 Contracted services must be supported by a written agreement that describes deliverables benefiting the Program.

Section 5. Allocation

5.1 Costs that benefit both this subaward and other activities must be allocated in proportion to the benefits received, consistent with 2 CFR 200.405.

5.2 Costs may not be shifted to this subaward to overcome funding shortfalls on other awards.
